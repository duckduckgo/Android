# DuckDuckGo Privacy Browser (Android)

A Jetpack Compose & Kotlin privacy-focused Android browser built with Material Design 3, Room, DataStore, and Android WebView.

## Features

- **Private Search & Omnibar**: Smart URL vs. search query detection, live autocomplete (bookmarks, history, and DuckDuckGo suggestions), and configurable top or bottom omnibar placement.
- **Tracker & Ad Blocking**: Request-level blocking of third-party advertising, analytics, social, and fingerprinting domains with per-site protection toggles and a live Privacy Dashboard.
- **HTTPS Upgrades & Link Cleaning**: Automatic HTTP-to-HTTPS upgrades, tracking query parameter stripping (`utm_*`, `fbclid`, `gclid`, etc.), and Global Privacy Control (`Sec-GPC: 1`) signaling.
- **Multi-Tab & Fire Tabs**: Grid and list tab switcher, Fire Tabs (non-persistent storage & history excluded), and one-tap Fire Button clearing of tabs, cookies, cache, and history while preserving Fireproof Sites.
- **Bookmarks, Favorites, History & Downloads**: Local Room persistence for bookmarks, pinned New Tab Page favorites, searchable history, find-in-page, desktop site mode, page sharing, printing, and download tracking.
- **Appearance & Customization**: System, Light, and Dark themes with bundled Plus Jakarta Sans and JetBrains Mono typography.
